About FunPower Events

It began with our founder, Valentyna, who had a background in psychology and two young kids of her own — and felt like the local party scene wasn't offering anything special. So she put on a fairy costume and threw one memorable afternoon for a handful of neighborhood kids. Word got around fast, and pretty soon we were showing up with foam machines, laser tag gear, and a crew that handles setup and breakdown so nobody's parent is the one blowing up an inflatable at 8am. Five services and 3,000+ events later, we're still the same Bay Area crew that started with one fairy costume and a good idea.

Safety First

Every entertainer is background-checked, insured, and trained on our gear before they ever run a party solo.

Full-Service Setup

We arrive with everything — inflatables, gear, safety equipment — and handle setup and breakdown so you don't lift a finger.

Made For Every Age

From toddlers to pre-teens, our activities flex to fit the group, so every guest walks away buzzing.
