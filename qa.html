Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know before booking your party

Booking

Simply fill out our booking form or give us a call or text. We'll confirm your date, package, and any custom requests within 24 hours.

We recommend booking 2-4 weeks ahead, especially for weekends and holiday season, though we'll always try to accommodate last-minute requests.

Pricing & Packages

Pricing depends on the package, duration, and add-ons you choose. Reach out with your details and we'll send a custom quote.

Every package includes a professional entertainer, all activity equipment, setup and cleanup, and a full lineup of games tailored to your theme.

We accept Zelle, PayPal, Venmo, and cash. A deposit secures your date, with the balance due on the day of the event.

We don't offer discounts — every quote is priced fairly for exactly what's included, whether you're booking one activity or several.

Party Logistics

Most of our packages run 90-120 minutes, with some extending up to 190 minutes, fully packed with games, activities, and entertainment. We can also customize the length for your event.

Our team typically arrives 15-30 minutes early to set up and is fully packed up shortly after the party ends — we don't need any special space or extra time to get ready.

We can fit almost any space — backyard, front yard, clubhouse, restaurant, open field, beachside, or public park. Just share the details with us so we know how to tailor everything to your spot.

Both! Our team adapts activities to your backyard, a park, or an indoor venue — whatever works best for your celebration.

We keep an eye on the weather with you and can adapt activities to run wherever you'd like — we've even hosted parties in the rain before!
